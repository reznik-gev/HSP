Administrators set HSP up and keep it current: the buildings and their plans, the people and teams, and who sits where. A good order for a new installation is the one below.

## 1. Sites, buildings and floors

On [Floors](/floors), **Add site** creates a site, such as a city or a campus. Each site's **⋯** menu has **Add building**, and each building's has **Add floor**. The same menus **Edit** a name or **Archive** what's no longer in use.

> A building's code, such as HT, is short and unique within its site. It's used in seat labels and imports.

## 2. Draw a floor

Open a floor and click **Edit floor**. While you edit, the floor is locked, so nobody else can edit it at the same time. Your changes are saved as you go, as a draft that only administrators see.

Pick a tool on the left, or press its key:

- **Select** `V`: click to select, drag to move.
- **Wall** `W`: click corner by corner. `Enter` or `Esc` ends the wall.
- **Door** `D` and **Window** `N`: click on a wall.
- **Column** `C`, and **Zone** `Z` for an area such as an open space or a meeting room.
- **Place item** `P`: desks, chairs, cabinets and devices from the catalog.
- **Number seats** `L`: click seats in order, or drag across them, to label them.

![Drawing walls](draw-walls.gif)

The line under the plan always says what you can do next. Press `?` for **Keyboard and mouse**, the full list of keys and mouse moves.

Some things that save time:

- Type a number while drawing a wall for an exact length, or while dragging for an exact distance.
- The arrow keys nudge the selection by 1 cm, or 10 cm with `Shift`.
- `Shift`+drag selects everything inside a rectangle.
- **Array…** in Properties copies the selection in rows and columns, such as a row of desks.
- `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+V` copy and paste, on this floor or another one.
- `Ctrl+Z` undoes, and `Ctrl+Y` redoes.

![Placing and numbering desks](place-desks.gif)

**Properties**, on the right, shows the selection's details: a seat's label, a wall's length, an item's height. **Problems** lists what needs fixing, such as a seat without a label. Click a problem to find it on the plan.

## 3. Publish

When the floor is ready, click **Publish**. HSP shows what changed since the published version, and any assigned seats your changes would free. Add a note if you like, then publish. Everyone sees the new plan within 30 seconds.

![Publishing a floor](publish.gif)

**Discard draft** throws your changes away. **Version history** lists every published version, and can bring an older one back as a draft.

## 4. People and teams

Add teams on [Teams](/org) with **New team**. See them as a tree or as an org chart. Add people on [People](/people) with **New person**. Each person has one main team, and may belong to others.

For many people at once, use [Imports](/imports): pick **Teams**, **People** or **Seat assignments**, click **Download template**, fill it in, and choose the file. HSP shows a preview first. Nothing changes until you click **Apply import**. Import teams before people, and people before seats.

## 5. Assign seats

Click a seat on a floor's plan. **Assign** gives a free seat to a person, or reserves it for a team. **Change** and **Unassign** do the rest. Each change can be undone for a few seconds.

![Assigning a seat](assign-seat.gif)

On [People](/people), **Without a seat** lists the people who don't have one yet. Archiving a person, or removing a seat from the plan, frees the seat on its own.

## 6. The catalog and devices

The [Catalog](/catalog) holds the furniture and devices you place in the editor, with their sizes, and the **Zone types**, such as meeting room or kitchen. [Devices](/devices) lists each device by its asset tag: who it's issued to, and where it's placed. **Show on plan** finds it on its floor.
