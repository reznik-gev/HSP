HSP shows your organisation's offices: every site, building and floor, drawn as a plan, and who sits where. This part is for everyone. Administrators also see a second part, **For admins**, further down.

## Find where someone sits

Type a name, a team or a seat label in the search box at the top, **Where does… sit?** (or press `/` to jump to it). Press `Enter` to see every seat that matches, then **Show on plan** to open the floor with that seat picked out.

![Finding where someone sits](find-a-seat.gif)

You can also search from [Seats](/seats).

## Read a floor plan

Open [Floors](/floors) and pick a floor. Each seat is coloured by what it is:

- **Assigned:** someone sits here.
- **Reserved for a team:** kept for one team.
- **Free:** nobody sits here yet.
- **Bookable:** booked by the day, not assigned to anyone.
- **Unavailable:** out of use for now.

The colours are explained under the plan. Click a seat to see who sits there and their team.

To move around, drag the plan, and zoom with the scroll wheel or the `+` and `−` buttons. The button under them fits the whole floor on the screen. **Height**, under the plan, shows only what stands on the floor, or only what hangs above it.

## Find a free seat

On a floor's plan, **Show free seats** makes the free and bookable seats stand out. The number tells you how many there are.

![Showing the free seats on a floor](free-seats.gif)

To look across every floor, open [Free seats](/seats/free), under Seats. Narrow the list by site, building, floor, zone and kind, and use **Show on plan** to see where a seat is.

## Language and appearance

The two buttons at the top, beside your initials, change the **Language** (English or Hebrew) and the **Appearance** (light, dark, or the same as your computer). HSP remembers your choice.

## Your account

Click your initials at the top for your name and role, this guide, and **Sign out**. The release of HSP you're using is shown at the bottom of the menu, and of the sidebar.
