# 0142 — No Auto-fix: the owner starts each review round and says when to merge

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Refines:** [0134](0134-2026-11-09-from-request-to-main.md) (point 7: Auto-fix on every PR; point 8: up to three bot rounds, each reaching the session by Auto-fix), [0137](0137-2026-11-10-a-github-app-for-claude-code-sessions.md) (point 4: the owner's approval on GitHub is the go-ahead to merge)

## Context
[0134](0134-2026-11-09-from-request-to-main.md) has a session turn on Auto-fix for every PR it opens, without asking. Auto-fix is the Claude desktop app's switch that wakes a session on CI failures, merge conflicts and review comments. [0137](0137-2026-11-10-a-github-app-for-claude-code-sessions.md), [0139](0139-2026-11-10-how-sessions-get-the-apps-tokens.md) and [0140](0140-2026-11-10-the-token-helper-moves-to-gevtools.md) moved local sessions to push and open PRs as the owner's GitHub App, `reznik-gev-claude[bot]`.

On [#161](https://github.com/reznik-gev/HSP/pull/161), on 2026-11-11, the app refused to turn Auto-fix on: "GitHub does not list the account gh is signed in with on github.com as the PR's author; the user can turn on Auto-fix for it themselves." What's true today, checked on 2026-11-11:
- The app's `set_monitor` tool turns Auto-fix on only for a PR opened by the account the app's own `gh` is signed in with, which is the owner's. No app setting changes that.
- The owner can still turn Auto-fix on by hand from the PR bar.
- Without Auto-fix, the review bot's comments and CI failures don't wake the session.

The owner said why they chose as they did: running several sessions at once, they felt they were losing control of them. They want the review loop to move on their word, not on its own. At first the choice covered only PRs opened as the App. In review of [#163](https://github.com/reznik-gev/HSP/pull/163), the owner widened it to PRs opened with their own account too, and described the flow they want:
- The owner says the bot or the owner left comments. The session addresses them and prepares the PR for another review. This loops as many times as the owner and the session feel is needed.
- The owner says the PR is approved and ready to merge.

## Options considered
What a session does after it opens a PR:
- **No Auto-fix, for every PR. The owner tells the session when to go on** ✅. The owner says "prepare #N" when the bot's review is in, and tells the session about CI failures. Nothing moves without the owner, which is what they want, and every PR works the same way. The cost: the owner keeps watch over the PR on GitHub.
- No Auto-fix for App PRs only; PRs opened with the owner's account keep it, since the app accepts them. Two ways of working, and the loop would still run on its own for some PRs.
- The session asks the owner to turn Auto-fix on from the PR bar. One click per PR, then everything works as before. Claude recommended it. The owner set it aside: the loop would run on its own again.
- Open PRs with the owner's account, so Auto-fix works. That undoes [0137](0137-2026-11-10-a-github-app-for-claude-code-sessions.md).

## Decision
1. **A session doesn't turn on Auto-fix for any PR it opens,** as the App or with the owner's account, and doesn't ask the owner to. It still binds the PR to the session (`get_status`, then `bind_pr`). *(The owner's choice)*
2. **The owner starts each review round.** The owner says the bot or the owner left comments on the PR, or "prepare #N", and the session runs [`prepare-pr-for-review`](../../.claude/skills/prepare-pr-for-review/SKILL.md). It addresses the comments, then comments `@claude review` itself, reports, and stops. It goes on when the owner starts the next round. *(The owner's choice)*
3. **No limit on rounds.** 0134's "up to three bot rounds" and the session's own stop when a round brings only nitpicks go: the owner decides when the PR is done. *(The owner's choice)*
4. **The owner's comments are addressed like the bot's,** since the owner asked for the round: each is a fix, a disagreement or a choice. The session replies to each one it fixed, but leaves resolving it to the owner. *(Claude's choice: the owner wrote the thread, so the owner closes it)*
5. **CI failures:** the session reports CI's state when it opens the PR and after each push. After that, the owner tells it when CI fails. *(The owner's choice)*
6. **The go-ahead to merge is the owner's word in the session:** the PR is approved and ready to merge, or "merge #N". The session merges with [`merge-pr`](../../.claude/skills/merge-pr/SKILL.md). It doesn't merge on an approval it wasn't told about. A PR opened as the App still needs the owner's approval on GitHub first, since the App can't merge without it ([0137](0137-2026-11-10-a-github-app-for-claude-code-sessions.md) point 3). *(The owner's choice)*
7. **When a session opens a PR, its report says** that Auto-fix is off, and that the owner says "prepare #N" when the bot's review is in. *(Claude's choice)*

## Consequences
- [`open-pr`](../../.claude/skills/open-pr/SKILL.md) step 5 and its report, [`prepare-pr-for-review`](../../.claude/skills/prepare-pr-for-review/SKILL.md) (when it runs, the owner's comments, steps 6 and 7), [`merge-pr`](../../.claude/skills/merge-pr/SKILL.md) step 1, the [git guide](../guides/git.md), and [How a change is made](../guides/making-a-change.md) steps 6, 7 and 9 change to match.
- The owner can still turn Auto-fix on by hand from the PR bar for a PR they want to run on its own.
- **To consider later:** if the owner finds the watching too much, the option set aside above (one click per PR) is the next step.
