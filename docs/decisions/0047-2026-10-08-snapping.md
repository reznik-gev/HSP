# 0047 — Snapping: grid + object/wall snap

- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Status:** Accepted

## Options considered (multi-select)
- **Grid snap** ✅
- Angle snap ❌ (not in v1)
- **Object / wall snap** ✅
- Alignment guides ❌ (not in v1)

## Decision
**Grid snap:**
- The grid size is selectable: 1, 5, 10 or 50 cm. The default is **5 cm**, and the choice is stored per user.
- `G` toggles grid snap. Holding `Alt` temporarily disables all snapping.
- The grid is drawn adaptively, with major and minor lines that fade with zoom.

**Object / wall snap** (higher priority than grid when within ~8 screen px):
- Snap targets: wall endpoints, wall midpoints, wall centrelines (creating T-junctions per [0044](0044-2026-10-08-wall-joins.md)) and wall faces.
- **Face snap:** an object snaps flush against a wall face and auto-rotates to face away from the wall, for example a desk's back against the wall.
- **Attach snap:** items with `attaches_to` ([0021](0021-2026-10-08-object-catalog.md)) snap onto a compatible host (a monitor onto a desk) and record `attached_to`.
- Zone vertex snap: zone polygon vertices snap to wall faces and corners, so rooms follow walls.
- The active snap target is shown with a marker and tooltip ("Endpoint", "Wall face", …).

## Consequences
- Without angle snap, exact angles come from typed input (`350<90`, [0045](0045-2026-10-08-dimension-entry.md)). Drawing along the 0°/90° axes still works by snapping to the grid.
- Angle snap and alignment guides can be added later without model changes.
- Snapping is computed with a spatial index over the client plan store, which keeps it fast on large floors.
