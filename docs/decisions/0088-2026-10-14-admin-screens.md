## Undo
Undo replays the release report in reverse, using what the server returned:
- **Deactivating a person:** reactivates them and gives back the seats they held.
- **Unassigning a seat:** assigns it again.
- **Archiving a site, building or floor:** restores the items top-down and reassigns their seats.
- **Archiving a unit:** restores it and its sub-units, puts the moved members back, and reassigns unit-held seats.

**Undo never takes anything from someone else.** A seat that's been given to another person in the meantime is skipped, and the notice says so, rather than being reassigned silently.

## Phase 2 PR sequence (stacked, [0079](0079-2026-10-08-stacked-pr-workflow.md))
1. **Admin plumbing:**
   - knowing whether the user is an admin
   - the side-panel and form building blocks
   - notifications with Undo (the shadcn/ui *Sonner* toast, *lower-confidence*)
2. **People:**
   - the list (search; unit, active and without-a-seat filters)
   - the person panel: details, teams, seats, devices
   - create, deactivate and reactivate
   - a small backend filter for people without a seat
3. **Org:** the tree, the unit panel (members, positions, rename, move, archive) and the org chart.
4. **Seat assignment:** Assign and Unassign in the plan's seat panel, with the person or team picker.
5. **Imports:** units, people and seat assignments, with templates.
6. **Locations:** add, rename and archive sites, buildings and floors on the Floors page.
