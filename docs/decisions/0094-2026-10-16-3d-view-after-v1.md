# 0094 — Move the 3D view past v1; show heights in 2D instead

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Supersedes:** the 3D view in [0025](0025-2026-10-08-v1-scope.md) (v1 scope) and the 2D / 3D / Split modes in [0043](0043-2026-10-08-editor-view-layout.md) for v1
- **Refines:** [0089](0089-2026-10-15-floor-editor.md) (its 3c step), [0021](0021-2026-10-08-object-catalog.md)

## Context
[0025](0025-2026-10-08-v1-scope.md) put "a generated 3D view" in v1, and [0089](0089-2026-10-15-floor-editor.md) planned it for phase 3c with the layout from [0043](0043-2026-10-08-editor-view-layout.md). While planning 3c, the product owner looked again at what the 3D view is for. The need is to **show where things stand in height**: wall-mounted screens, shelves, and windows with their sills, so a plan shows where everything is. The plan model already stores all of it (an object's `z`, wall heights, sills).

A full 3D renderer is one way to show height. The options weighed:
- **Height cues on the 2D plan** ✅ (for v1): raised items drawn differently, with their height range; window sill and top heights; a filter by height.
- **Wall elevations** ❌: a straight-on drawing of one wall with everything on it, at true heights, editable. Judged an unnecessary middle step before real 3D.
- **The full 3D view** (as in [0043](0043-2026-10-08-editor-view-layout.md)): moved to a later release. It's the biggest single piece of the frontend (a new rendering stack, furniture models, a performance gate, picking in 3D) and not core to what v1 has to achieve.
- **A fake 3D (axonometric) drawing** ❌: nearly the cost of elevations, hard to read where things overlap, and you can't measure in it.

## Decision
1. **v1 has no 3D view.** Heights are shown on the 2D plan ([0095](0095-2026-10-16-heights-catalog-and-devices.md)).
2. **The full 3D view comes in a later release, directly, with no elevation views in between.** [0011](0011-2026-10-08-frontend-stack.md) (react-three-fiber) and [0043](0043-2026-10-08-editor-view-layout.md)'s behaviour (follows the selection, shows unsaved edits, orbit and walk-through, 2D stays the only place to edit) stay the target for it.
3. Answers already given for that release, to start from when it's planned:
   - **Built-in items are drawn as simple models built from parts in code** (a desk is a top and legs, a chair a seat, back and post, a monitor a screen on a stand), stretched to the item's exact size. Custom items are drawn as their stored shape. No model files, so no art or licences to maintain. This replaces [0021](0021-2026-10-08-object-catalog.md)'s "bundled low-poly 3D model" per built-in item.
   - **3D is offered on the floor page too**, read-only, for everyone, not only in the editor.

## Consequences
- v1 ships sooner, without three.js and its download.
- Nothing in the data model changes: heights, sills and `z` are already stored and edited in the properties panel, so 3D can be added later without migrations.
- Until then, people who aren't editors see floors only from above, with the height cues.
- [0032](0032-2026-10-08-architectural-elements.md)'s "the 3D view extrudes walls and cuts openings" describes the later 3D view.
