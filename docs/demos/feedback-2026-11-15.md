## 1. Keeping seating data up to date
**Noted:** the client's main concern. They showed an earlier tool in their organisation with the same idea. It drifted from reality because people didn't use it consistently.

**The owner's answer:** team leads should have a reason of their own to use HSP, for example to manage their team's PCs and other items. Then they keep it current, and it's less likely to drift.

**Today:** only admins can change anything: plans, seats, people and devices ([0027](../decisions/0027-2026-10-08-v1-interim-people-and-access.md), part 2). Unit managers are planned ([0024](../decisions/0024-2026-10-08-unit-managers.md)): a manager manages their unit and its sub-units ([0007](../decisions/0007-2026-10-07-authorization-model.md)). Nothing lets them edit yet. Devices keep their tag and history, and can be issued to a person ([0022](../decisions/0022-2026-10-08-device-asset-data.md)).

**To discuss:**
- What a team lead can do on their own: assign their team's seats, move their people, manage their team's devices? This is the first step past admin-only editing.
- What else keeps the data current: AD sync for people and teams (1.1, [0108](../decisions/0108-2026-10-21-first-client-demo-feedback.md)). Seats are already freed when a person is deactivated ([0086](../decisions/0086-2026-10-12-automatic-release.md)). With AD sync, someone who leaves is deactivated without an admin doing it.
- Something that shows drift: for example "last checked" per team, or a reminder to a team lead to confirm their team's seats.

## 2. Importing plans from AutoCAD, Visio and images
**Noted:** very important to this client. HSP should read AutoCAD and Visio files, and PNG or JPEG images. The owner sees this as needed as soon as possible, to get the first customer using HSP.

**Today:** plans are drawn from scratch, with no background image to trace over ([0048](../decisions/0048-2026-10-08-no-plan-underlay.md)). The friend's demo raised the same point ([2026-10-25, point 2](feedback-2026-10-25.md#2-drawing-every-floor-costs-too-much-up-front)), and it isn't decided yet. 0048's revisit trigger, "if customer onboarding is the bottleneck", is now met twice.

**What each format means:**
- **PNG or JPEG:** a picture. HSP can show it under the plan to trace over, at a scale set from two points of known distance. Turning it into walls by itself is unpredictable.
- **AutoCAD (DWG or DXF):** real lines and blocks. Walls and desks could come in as real elements, after a step that says which layer is what. DWG is a closed format: reading it needs a converter or a paid library, while DXF is open.
- **Visio (VSDX):** shapes on a page. It's an open format, but office plans in Visio vary a lot. Exporting to PDF or an image and tracing over that may be enough.

**To discuss:** order and release. A likely path: tracing over an image or PDF first (cheap, works with every format after an export), then DXF, then DWG and Visio if clients need them. Does this come before AD sync (1.1)?

## 3. Big moves and possible seating arrangements
**Noted:** big moves and possible seating arrangements need to be part of HSP more than we thought. We need to think about how to build this properly.

**Today:** [0145](../decisions/0145-2026-11-11-office-moves.md) decided a move planner in 1.3: pick people or a team, place them on new seats, see before and after, and apply it all at once. Devices follow their person. Furniture rearranging, with "future layouts" prepared ahead of moving day, comes after it.

**To discuss:**
- Is 1.3 too late?
- "Possible arrangements": several drafts of a move side by side, to compare and pick one? 0145 doesn't cover this. The planner's screens are still to be designed.
- Do big moves need furniture changes and seat changes together in the same plan, rather than one after the other?

## 4. Fixing org data after an import
**Noted:** managing the org must be easy, and must allow fixing import mistakes. The client's data may not be complete or correct.

**Today:** admins can edit what an import brings in ([0084](../decisions/0084-2026-10-10-people-org-api-and-import.md)). On **Teams**: rename a team, move it under another parent, archive it, and edit members and positions. On **People**: edit a person, change their teams and which one is primary, and deactivate them. Imports match records on `external_id`, so fixing the source file and importing again also works. When AD sync comes, records it matches become read-only in HSP ([0027](../decisions/0027-2026-10-08-v1-interim-people-and-access.md), part 1).

**To discuss:**
- Which mistakes this client expects: wrong team, duplicate people, missing teams, a wrong hierarchy?
- Is there anything you can't fix today, such as merging two duplicate people?
- After AD sync, how are AD's mistakes fixed if its records are read-only in HSP: in AD only, or with overrides kept in HSP?

## 5. Searching for a person without a seat
**Noted:** searching for a person should always lead somewhere. If they have no seat, show where their team sits instead, or their next smallest org unit that has seats.

**Today:** "Where does… sit?" searches seats ([0085](../decisions/0085-2026-10-12-seat-assignment-api.md), part 5). It matches a seat's label, its holder's name or email, the team it's reserved for, and the holder's primary team. A person with no seat gets no result for their own name.

**To discuss:**
- What to show: the seats of their primary team, the team's area on each floor, or both?
- How far up to go: the primary team first, then its parent, up to the first unit with seats?
- How the result says it: "Dana has no seat. Platform team sits here."
