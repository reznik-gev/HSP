# 0024 — Unit managers: assigned in HSP only

- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Refines:** [0019](0019-2026-10-08-org-unit-source.md) (the optional `managedBy` derivation is **not** used)

## Options considered
- AD `managedBy` plus extra managers in HSP
- **HSP only** ✅
- AD only

## Decision
A unit's **managers are assigned in HSP** by admins or by managers of an ancestor unit. AD only provides structure and membership.

## Consequences
- Clear split: **AD = who belongs where; HSP = who manages what.**
- A manager need not be a member of the unit (for example a facilities coordinator who manages several teams' spaces).
- Manager assignments survive AD restructuring as long as the unit's group ID persists. If the unit is archived ([0019](0019-2026-10-08-org-unit-source.md)), its manager grants are suspended and reported.
- Managers inherit downward per the [0007](0007-2026-10-07-authorization-model.md) model: a manager of a unit manages its sub-units.
- Bootstrap: the first tenant admin is designated during installation (configured in Keycloak or HSP setup) and assigns the first managers.
