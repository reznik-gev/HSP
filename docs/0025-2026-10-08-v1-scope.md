# 0025 — v1 scope: space modeling + seat assignment

- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Status:** Accepted

## Options considered (multi-select)
- **Space modeling + 3D view** ✅
- Org sync + permissions ❌ (deferred)
- **Seat assignment** ✅
- Seat booking ❌ (deferred)

## Decision
**In v1:**
- Sites, buildings and floors. A 2D editor with cm precision, zones, built-in and parametric catalog items, light asset fields ([0004](0004-2026-10-07-space-authoring-model.md), [0020](0020-2026-10-08-space-hierarchy.md)–[0022](0022-2026-10-08-device-asset-data.md)).
- A generated 3D view.
- Draft → publish versioning and the exclusive edit lock ([0015](0015-2026-10-08-floor-plan-versioning.md), [0016](0016-2026-10-08-edit-concurrency.md)).
- Permanent seat assignment to people or units, "where does X sit?", and occupancy views ([0009](0009-2026-10-07-seat-assignment-model.md), assigned mode only).

**Deferred to a later release:**
- AD/Keycloak sync of people and unit groups ([0017](0017-2026-10-08-people-data-source.md), [0019](0019-2026-10-08-org-unit-source.md), [0023](0023-2026-10-08-primary-unit-rule.md)).
- Delegated space ownership via OpenFGA ([0007](0007-2026-10-07-authorization-model.md), [0013](0013-2026-10-08-rebac-engine.md), [0024](0024-2026-10-08-unit-managers.md)).
- Seat booking ([0009](0009-2026-10-07-seat-assignment-model.md), bookable mode).

## Consequences
- The deferred decisions remain **Accepted** as the target architecture. v1 must not contradict them: the `tenant_id`, external-ID columns, seat `allocation_mode` and the `space`/`org_unit` model all exist from day one.
- Seat assignment needs **people (and probably units) to exist in v1**, and some form of **authorization**. How v1 covers these without the deferred sync and ReBAC is decided in the next record.
